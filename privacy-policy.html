Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Learn how Nextralis collects, uses, and protects your information.

In short

  • We collect only what the features need: your account details, resumes you upload, your interview answers, payment records, and messages you send us.
  • Your resume text and interview answers are sent to an AI service so it can write your analysis and feedback.
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay. We do not receive or store your card or UPI details.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your data through our Contact Us page.

What We Collect and Where It Goes

  • Register: your name, email, and password. They are saved in our database, and your password is stored only as a one-way hash, so we cannot read it.
  • Log in: your email and password, which are checked against the stored hash. After you log in, your browser keeps a sign-in token and basic account details in its local storage.
  • Forgot or reset password: your email, then a new password. We email a reset link to your address. We store a hash of the reset token, which is valid for one hour, and the new password as a hash.
  • Profile: your name, bio, and any password change. These are saved on your account. Your email is shown but cannot be changed on this page.
  • Resume analysis: your resume (PDF), target role, and optionally a job description. The PDF is saved on our server. We also store the text extracted from it, your role and job description, and the scores and analysis generated. The resume text and job details are sent to our AI provider.
  • Voice interview: your job role, experience level, interview type, difficulty, and answers. We store the questions, your answers or transcripts, feedback, scores, and timing. Answer text and interview context are sent to our AI provider.
  • Subscription and checkout: your plan, and your billing name, email, address, PIN code, and phone number. We store your plan and usage, billing details, amount, order and payment IDs, and payment status. Razorpay receives your name, email, and phone number to open its payment window.
  • Contact Us: your name, email, phone number, and message. They are saved in our database and emailed to our team so we can reply.
  • Whole site: your theme choice (light or dark), which is kept only in your browser.
  • The landing page and these legal pages do not collect anything from you. Dashboard and result pages show data we already hold and do not ask for more.

How We Use Your Data

  • Create and secure your account, and sign you in
  • Generate AI-powered resume analysis
  • Conduct mock interviews and create interview reports and recommendations
  • Take payments, activate your plan, apply usage limits, and send payment confirmations
  • Send account emails such as password resets
  • Reply to your questions and support requests
  • Find and fix problems on the platform

AI Processing

Resume analysis and interview feedback are generated by a third-party AI service. When you use these features, we send it the text of your resume, the role and job description you entered, and your interview answers with their context, only to produce your results. Your resume often contains your name, phone number, email, and work history, so please do not upload documents with details you are not comfortable sharing with an AI service, such as government ID numbers.

Voice and Speech

Nextralis does not send raw audio to our servers. If you answer by speaking, your browser may turn your speech into text using its own speech recognition, and depending on your browser that audio may be processed by the browser's maker under its own privacy policy. We receive and store only the resulting text.

Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay, and your card, UPI, or bank details are entered with Razorpay and are not received or stored by Nextralis. We keep the amount, order and payment IDs, payment status, and the billing details you give us at checkout so we can activate your plan, issue invoices, and handle refunds. Your plan is activated only after we verify the payment with Razorpay, and Razorpay's own privacy policy applies to the information it collects.

Who We Share Data With

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Our AI provider receives resume text, job details, and interview answers to generate your results.
  • Razorpay receives your name, email, phone number, and payment details to take payment.
  • Our email service handles your email address and message content to send password resets, payment notices, and replies.
  • We may disclose information to authorities where the law requires it.

How Long We Keep Data

  • Account, resume, interview, payment, and contact records are kept until you ask us to delete them. They are not deleted automatically.
  • Payment records may be kept longer where we need them for tax, accounting, or legal reasons.
  • Usage-limit records are deleted automatically about 90 days after their usage period ends.
  • Password reset tokens expire after one hour.

Data Security

Passwords and reset tokens are stored only as hashes, reset links expire after one hour, and plan access is granted only after server-side payment verification. No online service can be perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and log out on shared devices. If you think your account has been accessed without permission, contact us right away through our Contact Us page.

Browser Storage

Nextralis keeps your sign-in token, basic account details, and theme preference in your browser's local storage so that you stay signed in and your theme is remembered. Logging out or clearing your browser data removes the sign-in details.

Your Rights

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct information that is wrong (you can also edit your name and bio in your profile)
  • Ask us to delete your account and the data linked to it, including uploaded resumes and interview records
  • Withdraw your consent to processing, which may mean we can no longer provide parts of the service
  • To make a request, use our Contact Us page. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Children

Nextralis is meant for people preparing for jobs and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and its date. Last updated: 10 November 2026.
