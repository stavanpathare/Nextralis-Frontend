Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: 10 November 2026

Platform: Nextralis

Website: https://nextralis.netlify.app

1. Refund Eligibility

Nextralis follows a usage-based refund policy.

A customer may request a refund for a purchased subscription if the subscription has not been used. To determine eligibility, our team will verify the customer's subscription and usage records in our backend system.

A subscription may qualify for a refund if:

  • No resume has been submitted for analysis under the purchased subscription.
  • No voice interview has been started or processed under the purchased subscription.
  • No other applicable paid subscription benefits have been used.

Refund requests are reviewed individually and are subject to verification and applicable consumer-protection laws.

Once a paid subscription benefit has been used, the subscription will generally not qualify for a refund merely because the customer has changed their mind. This does not limit any refund rights available under applicable law.

2. How to Request a Refund

Customers can submit a refund request through the Contact Us page on the Nextralis website.

Please include the following information:

  • Registered email address.
  • Invoice number.
  • Razorpay Order ID or Payment ID.
  • Purchased subscription plan.
  • Reason for requesting the refund.

Our backend team will verify the payment, subscription details and usage records before communicating the refund decision.

3. Accidental Duplicate Payments

If a customer is charged more than once for the same subscription purchase, Nextralis will investigate the payment records.

After confirming that multiple successful payments were made for the same purchase, the duplicate payment will be eligible for a refund. The valid subscription activation will be retained.

4. Payment Successful but Subscription Not Activated

If payment is successful but the purchased subscription does not appear in the customer's account, please allow up to 24 hours for the activation process.

If the subscription remains inactive after 24 hours, contact us through the Contact Us page and provide your payment details.

Our team will verify the transaction and take appropriate action, which may include activating the purchased subscription or initiating a refund.

5. Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved and initiated, it may take up to 30 days for the amount to be credited to the customer's original payment method.

The actual processing time may depend on the payment provider, banking institution and payment method.

6. Unused Subscription Time

Unused subscription days, remaining interview quotas or other unused subscription benefits are not automatically eligible for a partial or prorated refund.

For example, if a customer purchases a 30-day subscription and uses it for 10 days, the remaining 20 days will not automatically qualify for a refund.

Refund requests will nevertheless be considered where required by applicable law or where Nextralis determines that a refund is appropriate.

7. Subscription Cancellation

Customers who wish to cancel their subscription may contact us through the Contact Us page.

Our team will verify:

  • Customer account and registered email address.
  • Invoice number and payment/order ID.
  • Purchased subscription plan.
  • Subscription activation and expiry dates.
  • Resume analysis usage.
  • Voice interview usage.
  • Remaining subscription quotas and other relevant usage records.

Based on this verification, our team will determine the appropriate cancellation and refund action.

Where a refund is approved, the subscription entitlement may be revoked or adjusted, and the refund will be initiated.

Cancellation does not automatically guarantee a refund. Eligibility remains subject to this policy and applicable law.

8. Automatic Payments and Renewals

Nextralis currently uses one-time payments for fixed subscription periods.

There are no automatic recurring payments or automatic subscription renewals. Customers must manually purchase a new subscription when their current subscription expires.

Therefore, customers do not need to cancel an automatic payment mandate when cancelling their subscription.

9. Contact Us

For refund requests, payment-related issues or subscription cancellation, please contact us through the Contact Us page on the Nextralis website.
